Research Experience

  • 2022–2024: Intelligent Operation and Maintenance Technology of Long-Span Hybrid Beam Rigid Frame Bridge Based on Vehicle-Bridge Correlation Prediction
    • Key Researcher — Science and Technology Project of Zhejiang Provincial Department of Transportation
    • Duties included:
      • Deep learning framework for real-time vehicle axle load position detection, integrated with a weigh-in-motion (WIM) system
      • Homography matrix estimation for the camera-to-road mapping based on lane geometric prior information, removing the need for on-site labelling
      • GAN-based detection of road and bridge cracks in small-sample scenarios
      • Nighttime crack detection based on image-to-image translation models
      • Weakly-supervised and unsupervised damage detection algorithms for scenarios with limited labelled data
  • 2022–2024: Intelligent Vehicle-Bridge Correlation Monitoring Technology of Long-Span Continuous Beam Bridge with Corrugated Steel Webs
    • Key Researcher — Science and Technology Project of Hebei Provincial Department of Transportation
    • Duties included:
      • Monocular 3D inference algorithm integrated with deep learning models for identifying the spatial-temporal distribution of vehicle wheel loads
      • Vehicle wheel load identification framework for dense heavy traffic using image inpainting models
      • Bridge strain high-frequency information extraction based on wavelet multiresolution analysis
      • Deep kernel regression Bayesian model for time-series forecasting of temperature-induced bridge strain
      • Vehicle load–bridge response correlation analysis and development of a bridge weigh-in-motion (B-WIM) system
