About Me

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Architecture and Civil Engineering at City University of Hong Kong. I received my Ph.D. in Civil Engineering from the Department of Bridge Engineering, College of Civil Engineering, Tongji University (2025), and was a visiting Ph.D. student in the Department of the Built Environment at the National University of Singapore (2023–2024), supported by the China Scholarship Council.

My research focuses on computer vision and machine learning for civil infrastructure, including structural health monitoring, vehicle load identification, vision-based damage detection, and time-series modeling of bridge monitoring data.

My work has been cited 348 times on Google Scholar with an h-index of 9 (as of September 2026).

Research Interests

Structural health monitoring · Digital twin · Computer vision · Machine / deep learning · Signal processing

  • Monocular 3D vision algorithms based on geometric priors (standardized 3D reconstruction, automated homography estimation)
  • Vision-based detection of structural and bridge damages under small samples or changing illumination
  • Vision systems for accurate spatial-temporal identification of vehicle loads in complex environments
  • Time-frequency feature analysis and blind source separation of bridge health monitoring signals
  • Integration of Bayesian methods and deep learning for time-series prediction of sensor data
  • Vehicle load inversion and structural condition assessment based on vehicle-induced responses
